The store section “Enhance Your Arsenal” is one sentence: customize your weapons and select unique loadouts. A later sentence pitches “the smoothest aiming, shooting, running, and gliding,” including on low-end machines. That is the weapons chapter of the Steam page. There is no arsenal table.

Guns below appear because a dated official post named them. Absence from this page does not mean a gun is bad or missing. Attachment names and the spelling “Bzion” are copied from the post, including where the wording looks like a typo.
</Callout>

## What to learn before a stat sheet

The store’s own combat line says guts and pursuit matter, and that camping a spot will not go unchallenged. Paired with gliding and squad play, a loadout is the gun you can keep on target while you move, not a spreadsheet rank.

Until a full list exists, use the select screen for four facts the HUD already has and this site does not:

1. The name your build shows.
2. Whether you can control the recoil for a full mag.
3. The range the mode is asking for. A Hot Zone hold and a battle-royale rotate are not the same fight. Mode rules that are actually printed are on [Modes](/modes).
4. The Striker skill you are pairing it with. VOX’s Sonic Overdrive, in the 1 Sep 2026 post, buffs weapon handling speed for you and nearby teammates. That is a gunplay buff, described on [Strikers](/strikers). Other pairings are unverified.

Class labels (assault rifle, SMG, sniper, and so on) are **not** in the Steam about text. Use the class word your client prints. Do not import one from a different shooter.

## Printed on 20 Oct 2026

The [20 Oct 2026 update](https://www.blood-strike.com/news/update/20261019/39377_1317020.html) groups the following under “Weapon Adjustments.” Cells are the post’s words. “Heavy Trigger” and the drum mag read as attachments sitting under a gun heading. The post does not say that in a legend, so treat the grouping as editorial.

| Heading in the post | What it prints |
| --- | --- |
| MP7 | Moderately increases reload speed. Heavy Trigger: +1 damage to all body parts. |
| AUG | Upper chest damage 23 → 25. Damage distance dropoff reduced. |
| Bzion | Reload speed slightly increased. The next line is “Bizon - 32-Round R.I.P Drum”: increased headshot damage, and a reload speed boost. Both spellings are in the post. One of them may be a typo. Check the client before you hunt for a gun named Bzion. |
| QBZ95 | Armor-Piercing Magazine: headshot damage increased; chest, abdomen, and limb damage reduced. |

No time-to-kill, fire rate, or mag size is printed except the “32-Round” in that Bizon attachment name. Do not extrapolate a DPS.

Skin names in the same post (Kar98k, MP7 - Netherflame, GALIL, M82, AR97, Spear, and others) are prize-pool outfits. They confirm those weapon names were worth a skin on that date. They are not balance changes, and this page does not rank them.

## G36, from 1 Sep 2026

The [1 Sep 2026 update](https://www.blood-strike.com/news/update/20260831/39377_1312689.html) calls the G36 a new weapon: “Type MM-99 209mm AU Assault Rifle,” described as steady suppression with a large drum magazine for medium-range fights. It says an exclusive BAS attachment, “Grenade Launcher,” switches to grenade mode in a match and fires high-explosive grenades. The unlock line is specific: level up the Strike Pass to unlock the G36 and that attachment.

That pass window is not re-stated in the 20 Oct post. **Whether the Strike Pass is still how you unlock the G36 on 23 Oct 2026 is unverified.** The “AU Assault Rifle” wording is the post’s, and it is the one place an official note used an assault-rifle label for a named gun.
