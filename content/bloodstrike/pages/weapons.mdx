## What to learn before a stat sheet

The store’s own combat line says guts and pursuit matter, and that camping a spot will not go unchallenged. Paired with gliding and squad play, a loadout is the gun you can keep on target while you move, not a spreadsheet rank.

Use the select screen for four facts the HUD already has:

1. The name your build shows.
2. Whether you can control the recoil for a full mag.
3. The range the mode is asking for. A Hot Zone hold and a battle-royale rotate are not the same fight. Mode rules that are actually printed are on [Modes](/modes).
4. The Striker skill you are pairing it with. VOX’s Sonic Overdrive, in the 1 Sep 2026 post, buffs weapon handling speed for you and nearby teammates. The 20 Oct post then cut that skill’s delayed-damage ratio from 30% to 25%. That is a Striker change, described on [Strikers](/strikers), not a gun rank. Other pairings are unverified.

## Printed on 10 Nov 2026

The [10 Nov 2026 update](https://www.blood-strike.com/news/update/20261109/39377_1319149.html) is the newest official update on the news index as of 18 Nov 2026.

Battle royale, in that post:

- The respawn weapon changes to the **M4A1**.
- A “Store Featured Weapons” section is added to the in-match store. Weapons cost **3,000**. The sentence does not name the currency. A later line in the same post mentions cash inside high-tier loot zones. That is not a statement that the 3,000 is cash.
- **Beat Grenade** is a new throwable. Players hit by it are forced to dance. The post says it can be picked up at regular loot spots. It is not a gun.
- On **15 November**, after the BRS20 season update, the post says elite weapon crates replace elite airdrops in traditional BR, high-tier loot zones are marked with blue circles, and the elite rotation brings in the Bow **Falcon - Pursuit** and the KAG-6 **Banshee - Overload**, both described as elite weapons with special attachments. The attachment part names are not printed. The Minigun **Close-In Defense Cannon** and the M16 **Silent Hunter** are temporarily removed. This page did not open a match after 15 Nov, so the live rotation is the post’s schedule, not a client check.

Weapon section of the same post:

- **Beretta 93R**, called a pistol and a close-range weapon. Triple Tap fires three shots with one trigger pull. Unlock it in the new Strike Pass **Graffiti Artist**.
- **KAG-6** weapon level cap raised to 100. New BAS attachment **19.5" Overheat Barrel**: it heats during sustained fire, and bullet damage rises as the barrel glows. Unlock the level or the attachment in Graffiti Artist.

The pass block prints the window **11/15–12/14**, which includes 18 Nov 2026, and a **520 Gold** refund at max level. The pass block itself highlights the FN2000 skin “Graffiti Art,” not the pistol. The pistol and the barrel are in the weapon section, which names the same pass. The armory screen was not opened.

Skin and melee names in that post confirm the words, not the stats: Scorpion, Scar, Origin-12, RPK, HK416, M4A1, AK47, UMP45, FN2000, Butterfly Knife, Thunder Lash, and Katar. Thunder Lash and Katar are called melee weapons. A skin name is not a balance change.

## G36, from 1 Sep 2026

The [1 Sep 2026 update](https://www.blood-strike.com/news/update/20260831/39377_1312689.html) calls the G36 a new weapon: “Type MM-99 209mm AU Assault Rifle,” described as steady suppression with a large drum magazine for medium-range fights. The drum’s round count is not printed. An exclusive BAS attachment, “Grenade Launcher,” switches to grenade mode in a match and fires high-explosive grenades. The unlock line is specific: level up the Strike Pass to unlock the G36 and that attachment.

That pass window is not re-stated in the 20 Oct post or the 10 Nov post. The 10 Nov pass is Graffiti Artist, and its weapon section names the Beretta 93R and the KAG-6 barrel, not the G36. **Whether the September Strike Pass is still how you unlock the G36 on 18 Nov 2026 is unverified.**

The same “Type MM-99 209mm AU Assault Rifle” wording is also used for the [HK416 on 10 Feb 2026](https://www.blood-strike.com/news/update/20260210/39377_1286343.html) and the [AK47 on 11 Aug 2026](https://www.blood-strike.com/news/update/20260810/39377_1310623.html). It is the posts’ label, not proof that three guns are one model. Check the client.

## Still unverified

These are the gaps the posts did not close. They stay blank on purpose.

- The full gun list. A name that never appears above was not copied from a tier list or from memory.
- Live damage, fire rate, and time-to-kill. The client stat bar wins. The 16 Apr 2026 post does say QBZ95 and M1887 received a higher aim-assist coefficient on mobile, and that HK416 ADS speed was slightly increased. It does not give numbers for that coefficient.
- Current unlocks for the older Strike Passes (Bunny Force, Velvetopia, Urban Pulse, Dark Take, and the March, July, and September passes). Graffiti Artist’s printed window includes 18 Nov 2026. The pass screen was not opened.
- Whether the 15 Nov elite rotation is what a match still drops. The post scheduled it. A crate was not opened for this check.
- Prices and craft costs, except the two figures the 10 Nov post does print: featured in-match weapons at 3,000 with no currency name, and 520 Gold back at the max level of Graffiti Artist. This site does not sell the Gold.
- The redeem-code menu. That check is on [Codes](/codes). It is still empty of a verified string.
