The store’s performance line says the game aims at smooth aiming, shooting, running, and gliding even on low-end machines. The minimum GPU is integrated-class hardware. That is a store claim, not a framerate this site measured.

## What to do first

1. Let the client finish its own opening. The [20 Oct 2026 update](https://www.blood-strike.com/news/update/20261019/39377_1317020.html) says the **New Player Academy** is live: replay out-of-match guidance with one tap, and view illustrated tutorials. That is the official catch-up tool. This wiki is the skim beside it.
2. Pick a Striker. Steam says each one has an active and a passive. If you want a kit that was actually written down, read [VOX](/strikers). Otherwise pick whoever the select screen marks as available. Unlock prices are not on the store page.
3. Queue a mode the store bothers to name: **Battle Royale**, **Squad Fight**, or **Hot Zone**. What those words mean, and what they do not mean, is on [Modes](/modes). Ranked versus casual is not defined in the Steam about text. The 11 Aug 2026 news line about “BR - TPS” mentioned Ranked and Custom Rooms for a limited time. Whether that queue exists on 23 Oct 2026 is unverified. If you do not see Ranked, you are not missing a hidden menu this guide knows about.
4. Stay with the squad. The store says finding friends, adding them, and jumping into a fight as a team is the intended path. Solo is not forbidden by that sentence. It is just not the pitch.
5. Do not camp a building and call it tactics. The store says tactical decisions matter, and that you should not expect to hold a spot unchallenged.

HUD icons, the settings path, and the sensitivity screen are not in the Steam about text. The 20 Oct post does mention one pad option: Auto Sprint starts Tactical Sprint when the left stick enters the trigger range. Everything else on the control screen is yours to read in the client.

## Drop, glide, revive

The official homepage, checked 23 Oct 2026, groups player topics under these labels: Special Movements, Airdrop, Parachute, Upgrade, Revive, Respawn, Tactical Support, Shop, Communication, In-match Missions, Upgrade Weapon, Combat Tips, Teamwork. They are labels, not articles this site copied. Steam’s feature text separately names **gliding**, and one store screenshot is a wingsuit over a city.

Read together, the game expects a drop (airdrop / parachute) and a glide, then a fight where revive and respawn are different words. **How long a downed teammate can be revived, and whether respawn is a mode rule or a battle-royale card, is not printed on those labels.** Do not take a number from a different battle royale and paste it onto Blood Strike.

“Upgrade” and “Upgrade Weapon” are two labels. The weapons page only repeats balance lines NetEase printed. It is not a shopping list.

## Friends on other platforms

Steam’s category list includes Cross-Platform Multiplayer, Online PvP, and Online Co-op. The official homepage links:

- Google Play, package `com.netease.newspike`
- Apple App Store, id `1637059453`
- A PC download on NetEase’s easebar host
- The Steam page for AppID 3199170

The account-link clicks are not in the store about text. Open the client’s account or settings screen and follow that prompt. This guide will not guess the button order.

## Money, codes, and bans

In-app purchases exist. The 20 Oct 2026 post uses the word **Gold** for a login gift (30 Oct–1 Nov, “up to 999 Gold”) and for a gacha pull. Other posts mention more currency names. This page does not publish a shop price or a “best value” pack.

Redeem codes are a separate problem. As of 23 Oct 2026 this site has **no verified code**. See [Codes](/codes). Do not buy a “code bundle” from a top-up page because a list looks fresh. This site does not process top-ups or account recovery, and it is not responsible for bans or expired strings.
