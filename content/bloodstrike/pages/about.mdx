This is a short English wiki for **Blood Strike**, Steam AppID **3199170**, built as a static site for **bloodstrike.wiki**.

## Disclaimer

bloodstrike.wiki is an unofficial fan-made guide for *Blood Strike*. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by NetEase Games, Valve, or Steam**. All game content, names, artwork, and trademarks belong to their respective owners. Redeem codes expire and change without notice — verify in-game. This site does not process top-ups or account recovery. Not responsible for bans, expired codes, or purchase decisions.

Nothing here is account support. The address `bloodstrike@global.netease.com` is the support contact on the Steam store page and on the official site. Mail sent there reaches NetEase, not the editors of this wiki.

The [codes](/codes) page is a dated placeholder. It does not list a string you should paste. This website cannot redeem a code, restore a missed reward, or reverse a purchase.

Numbers that move — review scores, concurrent players, weapon tunings, event dates — are dated to 23 Oct 2026 or to the patch post they came from. If a page and your client disagree, trust the client.

<Callout kind="note" title="What “checked” means">
“Checked 23 Oct 2026” means the Steam API, the English store page, SteamCharts, and the linked official posts were read that day. It does not mean someone played every mode on that date.
</Callout>

## Images

Hero, capsules, headers, and screenshots are official Steam store assets for AppID 3199170, downloaded from the Steam CDN and served from this site so pages do not hotlink Steam. The file list and source URLs are in [ATTRIBUTION.txt](/images/official/steam/ATTRIBUTION.txt).

## Privacy, analytics, and ads

The site is static. It has no accounts and does not ask for your Blood Strike login.

`analytics.gaId` is `G-YZ05QF1DFN`. The base layout loads that GA4 measurement id with gtag on every page, including 404.

An Adsterra Native Banner named `afterQuickAnswer` loads once, immediately under Quick Answer, on the homepage and on guide pages. The script is the zone Adsterra issued for bloodstrike.wiki. The 404 page does not include the slot. Adult and sensitive categories stay off.
