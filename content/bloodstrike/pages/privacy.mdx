This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to **bloodstrike.wiki**, an independent, unofficial fan guide for *Blood Strike* (Steam AppID `3199170`). It does not govern the game client, NetEase Games, or Valve Corporation.

## 1. Static Site & Zero Account Collection

**bloodstrike.wiki** is compiled as static HTML, CSS, and client-side calculator scripts. We do not offer user registration, comment sections, or checkout forms, and we **never** ask for your NetEase login, email password, or Blood Strike User ID (UID) on this domain. All Strike Pass Gold calculations run locally inside your browser tab without transmitting your inputs to a server.

## 2. Analytics & Cookies (`GA4`)

We load **Google Analytics 4** (`G-YZ05QF1DFN`) via `googletagmanager.com/gtag/js` to understand aggregated page views, device viewports, and outbound partner link clicks (`affiliate_click` events). Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (such as `_ga` and `_ga_*`) in your browser to distinguish anonymous sessions.

- You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings, or install the [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
- Learn how Google processes analytics data on partner sites at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 3. Display Advertising Status

Third-party display advertising in `sites/bloodstrike/ads.config.ts` is currently **disabled** (`enabled: false`). No external banner or pop-under ad network scripts execute when you browse **bloodstrike.wiki**.

## 4. Affiliate Links & External Websites

Pages such as `/top-up` and `/codes` include marked affiliate links (`rel="sponsored noopener"`) to third-party UID recharge platforms such as **LootBar**. Clicking an external link takes you off **bloodstrike.wiki**; once you leave our domain, the destination site's own privacy policy, cookies, and checkout terms apply. We may earn a referral commission on qualifying purchases at no additional cost to you.
