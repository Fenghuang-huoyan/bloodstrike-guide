## The three Steam names

| Mode | What is actually known | What is not |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Battle Royale | Named in the Steam short description. A Latin America tournament rulebook excerpt on the official homepage uses Battle Royale with squads of 4. That is a cup format (50 squads, three weekends), not a promise that public matches are 100 players. An 11 Aug 2026 news line added “BR - TPS” with Ranked and Custom Rooms for a limited time. | Public player count, circle times, ranked rules, whether third-person is still in the queue. |
| Squad Fight | Named in the Steam short description. The 20 Oct 2026 post writes “SF - Strike Map Pack” in a map list beside Hot Zone. This page reads SF as Squad Fight. If the client expands those letters differently, trust the client. | Round length, score limit, team size. |
| Hot Zone | Named in the Steam short description, and again as a playlist that will receive the Graffiti District map on 5 November 2026. | The objective. This page will not guess “stand in the hill.” |

Steam’s genre list also includes Racing and RPG, alongside Action, Adventure, Massively Multiplayer, Strategy, and Free To Play. Those are store genres. The about text does not describe a racing mode or an RPG progression track. Do not treat the genre row as a mode list.

Gliding is a locomotion line in the store feature text, not a separate mode. The homepage also lists Parachute and Airdrop as topic labels. See [Beginner guide](/beginner-guide).

## Named in the 20 Oct 2026 post

The [20 Oct 2026 update](https://www.blood-strike.com/news/update/20261019/39377_1317020.html) is the freshest official mode note read for this scaffold.

**Nightmare Trial.** Called new gameplay, on War Tide, with a nighttime Shutter Island. The post says searching or fighting earns Giggle Coins, the Clown rewards high ranks, and after a time the player ranked first in total Giggle Coins becomes the Clown’s spokesperson and receives his chainsaw. Special hunting missions are also “coming to War Tide.” Night maps are “coming” to Nightmare Trial and to Big Head. How much of that was already live on 20 Oct versus still rolling out is the post’s mix of present and future tense. Re-read the client.

**War Tide** is the place name those sentences use. The post does not define it as “the battle royale map” in a glossary. It is the name attached to Nightmare Trial and, in older posts, to other events. Treat it as an official place name, not as a measured map size.

**Graffiti District.** “Will be added” on **5 November** (the post is dated 20 Oct 2026, so this date is still ahead of 23 Oct) to: SF - Strike Map Pack, Hot Zone, Arms Race, Weapon Master, Team Deathmatch, and Free For All. The post calls it a graffiti neighborhood and says to watch the small connecting pipes. **Apex Arena** “has been added” to Weapon Master.

**Big Head.** A returning gameplay in the benefits list, with a challenge. Rules are not printed.

Playlist names in that map sentence — **Arms Race**, **Weapon Master**, **Team Deathmatch**, **Free For All** — are real strings in the post. One-line rules were not attached. Team Deathmatch and Free For All mean what those English words usually mean only as a hint. Confirm the score in the client.

The same post says a custom-room option, “Only Room Owner Can Invite,” was added. That is a lobby switch, not a mode.

## What to queue while learning

Use a store-named mode you can finish, with a Striker whose skill you have actually read. VOX is the one kit written up on [Strikers](/strikers). A gun the 20 Oct post bothered to tune is listed on [Weapons](/weapons), which is not the same as “use only those guns.”

Ranked rating, party size limits, and fill-versus-solo toggles are TODO. They were not in the Steam about text on this check.
