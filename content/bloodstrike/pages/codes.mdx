## How In-Game Rewards & Codes Work in Blood Strike

1. **Event Tab vs. Redeem Field**: In the November 2026 build, free weapon skins (Stamp Collection on `11/19`, Butterfly Knife rally on `11/20`, and RPK skin starting `11/27`) unlock directly inside the **in-game Event menu**—no coupon string required.
2. **Avoid Fake Code Generators**: Any third-party tracker listing “free 9,999 Gold” strings is fabricated. Note that older official posts also mention **7-digit squad team codes** and **anniversary invitation codes**, which are matchmaking/friend invites rather than item coupons.
3. **520 Gold Strike Pass Loop**: According to the [10 Nov 2026 official patch notes](https://www.blood-strike.com/news/update/20261109/39377_1319149.html), completing the **Graffiti Artist Strike Pass (`11/15–12/14`)** refunds up to **520 Gold**, letting active players roll that Gold straight into the next season pass.
