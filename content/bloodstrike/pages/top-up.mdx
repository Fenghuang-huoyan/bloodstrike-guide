## How to Find Your Exact Blood Strike UID

Before placing any Gold order, double-check your UID inside the client so your purchase does not land on someone else's profile:

1. Launch **Blood Strike** on PC (Steam) or mobile and tap your **Avatar / Profile** in the top-left corner of the lobby.
2. Locate the numeric **UID** printed right beneath your player name and use the **Copy** icon rather than typing digits by hand.
3. Confirm your **Server Region** (e.g., Global, Americas, SEA, MENA) matches any region selector on the checkout page.

## Payment Methods, Delivery Window & Common Failure Fixes

- **Supported Regional Payments**: Depending on your country, UID checkout hubs support international cards (Visa/Mastercard), PayPal, Apple/Google Pay, and local e-wallets where App Store cards are unavailable.
- **Expected Delivery Time**: Most UID Gold orders credit within **1 to 10 minutes**. If Gold does not appear immediately in the top-right counter, restart the game lobby or check your in-game **Mail** tab.
- **What if You Typed the Wrong UID?**: Once Gold is dispatched to an existing valid UID on NetEase's server, neither the platform nor NetEase can reverse and transfer those coins to another player. If the mistyped UID does **not** exist, the order typically fails automatically and can be refunded by customer support—contact the checkout platform's live chat immediately with your order number before delivery completes.
