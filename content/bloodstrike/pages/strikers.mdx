Steam’s short description is the contract for this page:

> Blood Strike offers a wide range of playable Strikers, each with a unique active and passive ability letting you deploy drones, shield walls and everything in between.

A second store heading says to pick a hero, each with a unique active and passive skill. That is the whole roster disclosure on the store page checked 23 Oct 2026. Drones and shield walls are examples in that sentence. They are not the names of two Strikers.

<Callout kind="note" title="Not a full roster">
Key art shows a squad. This page does not transcribe names off a JPEG. If the select screen and a patch post disagree, trust the select screen. Ability numbers below are what NetEase printed, not a measurement from a match played for this guide.
</Callout>

## How to read a kit

The store splits every Striker into two skills:

| Piece | What the sources actually say |
| --- | --- |
| Active | Unique per Striker. The store’s examples are deploying drones and shield walls. Exact buttons are not in the about text. |
| Passive | The store says each Striker has one. The about text does not define what “passive” means in the HUD. |
| Levels | The 20 Oct 2026 post refers to “Distortion Pulse (LV5)” on VOX. Skills can have levels. How you level them is not in that post. |
| Weapons | Separate. The store has its own “Enhance Your Arsenal” section for weapon customization and loadouts. See [Weapons](/weapons). |

The store also says tactical decisions matter, and that you should not expect to camp a spot unchallenged. A shield or a drone does not replace moving. Gliding, running, and shooting are named in the controls pitch on the same page.

## VOX, from the 1 Sep 2026 post

The [1 Sep 2026 update](https://www.blood-strike.com/news/update/20260831/39377_1312689.html) introduces **VOX** as an acoustic engineer and underground DJ from Medellín’s District 13. The post says he empowers teammates through sound and disrupts enemy tempo. It then prints two skills. The post’s labels are “Primary Skill” and “Secondary Skill,” which is a different pair of words from the store’s “active” and “passive.” This page does not force those labels to match.

**Sonic Overdrive.** Boosts movement speed and weapon handling speed for you and nearby teammates. Part of incoming damage is deferred. Once out of combat, remaining deferred damage is cleared, and HP lost during the effect is restored.

**Sonic Grenade.** Throws a grenade that emits pulses through walls, reducing enemy movement speed and weapon handling speed, and preventing sprinting.

The post does not print cooldown, radius, or duration on 1 Sep. Those gaps stay empty.

### What changed on 20 Oct 2026

The [20 Oct 2026 update](https://www.blood-strike.com/news/update/20261019/39377_1317020.html) says VOX’s gunfight advantage and disruption were too strong. It prints these changes:

| Skill | Printed change |
| --- | --- |
| Sonic Overdrive | Delayed damage resolution ratio 30% → 25% |
| Sonic Grenade | Interference duration per wave 3s → 2s. Slow effect 30% → 25% |
| Distortion Pulse (LV5) | View rotation speed reduction 50% → 20% |

Distortion Pulse is not described in the 1 Sep kit blurb above. The October post treats it as a level-5 effect that restricts camera movement. What ranks 1 through 4 do is **not printed**. Do not back-fill them.

The same October note says HANK, JET, and VOX skill indicators were optimized on low graphics. That confirms the names **HANK** and **JET** exist in the build the post is talking about. It is not a kit sheet. Their actives and passives are a TODO until a post or the client states them.

## Names this page will not pretend to know

Official shop lines in September and October name outfits such as ZERO - Odashadow, Ran - Rave, Jack - Top Banana, JET - Psycho Clown, and Hank - Mr. Iguana. Those are skin titles. They tell you a Striker by that name was in a prize pool. They do not say what the Striker does.

| Name | Active / primary | Passive / secondary | Status on 23 Oct 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| VOX | Sonic Overdrive (1 Sep post; ratios tuned 20 Oct) | Sonic Grenade (1 Sep post; duration and slow tuned 20 Oct). Distortion Pulse LV5 is a separate October line. | Printed. Not re-tested in client. |
| HANK | Not printed | Not printed | Named 20 Oct 2026 in a graphics note only. |
| JET | Not printed | Not printed | Named 20 Oct 2026 in a graphics note only. |
| Everyone else | — | — | TODO. Do not trust a tier list that fills these cells without a date. |

Unlock cost, whether a Striker is free, and the order they appear in the select screen are not on the Steam page. The 20 Oct post’s “Eternal Striker skin” wording is about an outfit, not a price for the base hero.

Squad play is the other half of the store pitch: find friends, add them to a squad, and take a fight as a team. VOX’s overdrive is explicitly a nearby-teammate buff in the September post, which is a reason to stack rather than a reason to wander off. Mode rules are on [Modes](/modes).
